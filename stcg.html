How to Play 📖

Game Preparation

Before starting your match, ensure both players are ready.

1

Build a deck of exactly 30 unique cards.

2

Draw 5 cards for your starting hand.

3

Flip a coin to determine who goes first.

Draw Phase

Draw 1 card from your deck to start your strategy.

Play Phase

Deploy 1 Squirrel card into your active field.

Action Phase

Use skills or attack your opponent's cards.

End Phase

Discard excess cards and pass the turn.
